# NOVELTY / PRIOR-CONCEPT AUDIT — FINAL (10-gate QC, Gate 1)

## Claim tested
"A joint comparative representation of planetary dynamical state built from
three separately maintained measurement objects — current activity, persistence
through geological history, and observational constraint."

## Prior-art space checked
- Comparative planetology syntheses (Stofan, Horst, Jakosky & Persson, Catling,
  O'Rourke, Johnson): narrative comparisons; no formal triple-object matrix.
- Thermal-evolution frameworks (Phillips & Russell-type, Sohl, Sotin): scalar
  thermal histories; no activity/persistence/observability decomposition.
- Magnetic/dynamo surveys (Lillis, Vervelidou, Weiss, Mighani): single-axis
  persistence evidence, cited as inputs, not frameworks.
- Habitability indices / Cobb–Douglas-style scoring (exoplanet indices):
  aggregate scalar scores — different structure, cited where relevant.
- Systems/ontology work for reservoirs (Wright): cited for the reservoir ontology.

## Findings
- No published framework found that (a) separates observational constraint as a
  first-class axis, (b) codes persistence through dated cessation intervals, and
  (c) applies the same 7-reservoir × 7-process ontology across bodies.
- Novelty claim in the manuscript is scoped ("we are not aware of…" style),
  not absolute-first — verified in the Introduction and Discussion wording.

## Citation ecology
- 60 references; coverage of all E1–E7 anchors plus measurement-object,
  comparative-synthesis, and methods literature. No citation padding found:
  every reference is cited at least once in body text (Gate-5 table).
- **Disposition: PASS — novelty claim defensible, citation ecology clean.**
