# NOVELTY_AUDIT.md — Phase 1

Focused audit (2026-09-26): planetary vitality/senescence/metabolism, geodynamic lifetime,
heat engines, interior–atmosphere co-evolution, network Earth-system approaches, comparative
planetology. See literature/novelty_matrix.csv.

## Answers to the six required questions

1. **What already exists?** Per-body coupled evolution models (e.g. Venus interior–atmosphere–
   redox, PSJ ac2580), comparative-planetology classification schemes (comparative planetary
   geography review), interior/heat-engine reviews across bodies (Surveys in Geophysics 2021;
   "Rocky Planets as Heat Engines"), per-process cessation ages (Mars dynamo, Lillis 2008;
   Vervelidou 2017), and Earth-only climate network analyses (moisture hubs, TWS networks).
   Pieces exist; the integrated comparative structure does not appear to.

2. **Is "planetary vitality" already operationalized?** Only in the Earth-sustainability
   domain (Briggs et al. 2015 "planetary vital signs"; 2023 Earth Vitality Index = 63.74).
   Not in comparative planetology/geodynamics. Decision: keep "vitality" as a guarded umbrella
   metaphor only; the measurable object is circulation architecture. Novelty risk from the
   terminology alone: low, but we must not oversell the word.

3. **Comparable body×process circulation matrix published?** None found. Existing comparative
   work is either qualitative review or per-process (e.g., dynamo cessation ages). No published
   matrix separating current activity / persistence / observability across bodies on a common
   process ontology was located.

4. **Asynchronous persistence tested comparatively?** Not found. Individual cessation ages are
   published (Mars dynamo ~4.1 Ga, lunar mare volcanism, Mercury smooth plains) but no explicit
   within-body persistence-spectrum comparison across multiple circulation subsystems was found.

5. **Reservoir-network fragmentation quantified across bodies?** Not found. Graph/network
   formalisms are used for Earth's climate system (moisture recycling networks, TWS
   connectivity) but not as a cross-body comparative descriptor of circulation architecture.

6. **Genuinely new, or new terminology?** The contribution that is genuinely new:
   (a) a common reservoir/process ontology operationalized as three separated matrices
   (activity, persistence, observability) across eight diverse bodies;
   (b) the explicit test of whether comparative dynamical state is one-dimensional;
   (c) within-body asynchronous persistence quantified against dating uncertainty;
   (d) reservoir-network connectivity as a comparative variable.
   NOT new: the notion that planets differ in activity; per-body thermal evolution;
   tidal energy importance; individual process histories. The manuscript must be framed as a
   measurement/framework contribution plus an empirical test, not as "discovering" that planets
   differ.

## Framing decision
Keep the planned design. Position against the heat-engine/interior-review lineage
("what drives activity") and per-body coupled models, and state plainly that those are
complementary: our contribution is a common comparative state-space representation plus a few
falsifiable structural findings — not a replacement for interior modelling.

## Risk register
- Reviewer may say "qualitative review in matrix clothing" → counter: quantitative subset,
  observability separation, falsifiable hypotheses, sensitivity analyses.
- "Cherry-picked bodies" → frozen set chosen a priori for architectural diversity; state this.
- Small n → descriptive/null comparisons only; no ML.
