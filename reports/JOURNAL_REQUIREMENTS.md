# JOURNAL_REQUIREMENTS.md — EPSL (Earth and Planetary Science Letters)

Retrieved 2026-09-26 (UTC) from the live Elsevier Guide for Authors:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/earth-and-planetary-science-letters/publish/guide-for-authors
(ISSN 0012-821X; Editorial Manager: https://www.editorialmanager.com/epsl/default.aspx)

## Journal fit / scope
- EPSL publishes concise, high-impact "Letters" of broad interest across the entire Earth and
  planetary sciences; focus on physical/chemical processes, evolution, and general planetary-science
  questions. Companion papers are discouraged.
- Article type to select at submission: **Letter** (not "Frontiers Paper", which is invite-only).

## Hard limits (current guide)
- **Main text: ≤ 6,500 words** (Introduction → Conclusions). Excludes abstract, figures, tables,
  captions, acknowledgments, references, appendices, supplementary files. Headings, in-text
  citations, and equations DO count.
- **Figures + tables combined: ≤ 10.**
- **References: ≤ 70.**
- **Abstract:** concise; EPSL convention is < ~500 words (single paragraph, no references).
- **Highlights: MANDATORY.** Separate editable file, filename containing "Highlights";
  3–5 bullet points, max 85 characters each including spaces. Must report real novel results/methods.
- **Graphical abstract:** optional but encouraged. Separate file; Elsevier specs
  (min 531 × 1328 px target region, readable at small size; TIFF/EPS/PDF/MS Office accepted).
- **Keywords:** required (typically ≤ 6).
- **Cover letter:** directed to the editor; importance, relevance, why EPSL; word/figure-limit
  exception requests and COI indications go here. Do not duplicate the abstract.

## Structure & formatting
- "Your Paper Your Way": a single Word or PDF file is acceptable for the initial submission;
  correct format is required at revision.
- Standard sections: Title page (title, authors, affiliations, corresponding author e-mail + postal
  address, present addresses), Abstract, Keywords, Main text (IMRaD-style),
  Acknowledgements/Funding, Competing interests declaration (required even if none),
  Author contributions (CRediT encouraged), Data availability statement, References,
  Figure captions, Tables, Supplementary material.
- **References:** Elsevier author–year (Harvard) style — in-text "(Author, year)" /
  "(Author et al., year)"; reference list alphabetical, with DOIs. Verify exact punctuation at
  revision formatting stage.
- **Figures:** readable typography at print size; EPSL accepts common formats
  (TIFF/EPS/PDF/PNG at revision; single-file PDF/Word acceptable at first submission).
  Captions listed in manuscript; all figures must be cited in text.
- **Supplementary material:** encouraged for extended data/methods; separate file(s), cited in text.

## Declarations / policies
- Declaration of competing interests — mandatory statement.
- Generative-AI-in-writing declaration required if applicable (Elsevier policy).
- Data policy: data availability statement required; datasets/code should be deposited in a
  public repository with a DOI/citable link. Our public repo (bougtoir/planetary_circulation)
  satisfies this.
- Ethics statements: N/A for this study (no human/animal subjects).
