# NOVELTY / PRIOR-CONCEPT HOSTILE AUDIT
Date: 2026-10-17 (UTC). Scope: does prior work already combine COMMON ONTOLOGY × MULTIPLE
BODIES × CURRENT ACTIVITY × PERSISTENCE × OBSERVABILITY × NETWORK CONNECTIVITY × TEST OF A
SINGLE ACTIVITY AXIS?

## Closest prior frameworks (ranked by overlap)

1. **Per-body interior–atmosphere–surface coupled evolution models** (e.g. Way & Del Genio
   2020 for Venus; Mars volatile-loss modelling). Overlap: coupled circulation thinking per
   body. Difference: single-body dynamical models, not a common comparative state-space; no
   persistence-spectrum or observability separation.
2. **Surveys/reviews of comparative interiors** (Sohl et al. 2022 Surv. Geophys.; "rocky
   planets as heat engines" literature). Overlap: cross-body energetic framing. Difference:
   driver-side review ("what powers activity"), no body × process evidence matrix, no
   falsifiable dimensionality test.
3. **Earth-system "planetary metabolism / vital signs / exergy" literature** (Briggs et al.
   2015; Earth Vitality Index 2023; Kleidon exergy framing). Overlap: vitality vocabulary,
   composite state indicators. Difference: Earth-only; produces scalar indices — precisely
   what this paper tests and finds incomplete. Manuscript uses the vocabulary only as umbrella
   metaphor — kept, never as index.
4. **Comparative planetology classification** (comparative planetary geography reviews;
   terrestrial vs icy-world typologies). Overlap: common attribute lists. Difference:
   qualitative typology; no measurement tiers, no cessation fractions, no connectivity.
5. **Per-process cessation chronologies** (Lillis 2008; Vervelidou 2017 — Mars dynamo; Byrne
   2016 — Mercury; Li 2021 / Braden 2014 — Moon; Weiss & Tikoo 2014 / Mighani 2020 — lunar
   dynamo). Overlap: the persistence inputs themselves. Difference: single process per study;
   no within-body cross-process spread statistic, no comparison across bodies.
6. **Earth climate-network analyses** (moisture-recycling networks, TWS connectivity).
   Overlap: network formalism on a planetary system. Difference: Earth-only, transport
   networks of a single planet's climate; not a reservoir-ontology comparison across bodies.
7. **Habitability architecture frameworks** (Cockell et al. 2016 and descendants).
   Overlap: checklist-style multi-factor comparison. Difference: habitability question
   (could life persist), not dynamical-state measurement; no activity/persistence/observability
   separation.
8. **Non-equilibrium thermodynamics / planetary heat-engine theory** (Kleidon; Lorenz).
   Overlap: planets as dissipative systems. Difference: thermodynamic theory without an
   empirical cross-body evidence matrix.
9. **Exoplanet comparative-science framings** (habitable-zone demographics). Overlap:
   motivation for common state descriptions. Difference: no Solar-System evidence matrix.

## Verdict
No located prior work performs the full combination. Each ingredient exists somewhere; the
joint construct — a frozen common ontology scored as three separated evidence objects, then
used to *test* the adequacy of a single activity axis — was not found.
